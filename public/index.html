Belonging

We encourage you to register your existing community on the platform. Once the request is submitted, it will transfer to the 'Pending Phase' where humans, algorithms and peer communities will assess, recognize & approve your submission. It is reasonable to expect one of the following recognition outcomes: Culture, Proto-Culture or Sub-Culture. Please note multiple recognition outcomes may apply to your culture at the same time.

Please provide us with the following information to the best of your ability. You may want to take some time and collect cultural material along with community members declarations before submitting. It will help us determine the scope of your community and set it akin to others.

Your Own Words

Some things about a culture can't be captured by documents alone. This is your space to tell us more. For example: What makes your culture unique in its values, rituals, or inside jokes? How do you gather — online forums, local meetups, annual events? What symbols, foods, music, or sayings are meaningful to you? Which other cultures do you overlap with, borrow from, or collaborate with? What stories, challenges, or milestones have shaped your group? In short: what are you all about? Your words help bring your culture to life within the Belonging system.
